Ladies and gentlemen. Boys and girls. Employers and admissions officers.

We did it.

The capstone project—a full security audit of the very chat app that started me off on this journey—is officially complete! In this post, as promised, you'll find the full 53-page report, as well as the GitHub repo for the chat app itself, if you're interested in taking a look at what 7th-grade me was able to achieve.

Before I get into that, I need to make a quick note: I used a lot of AI to help me compile that report. As I said a second ago, that report was 53 pages long—a report of that caliber could genuinely take a whole month to put together, especially with this being my first one. Seeing as college apps opened right around the time I finished the testing phase of the audit, I absolutely DID NOT have a full month to dedicate to the report alone. That would've been nearly 4 times the amount of time that the testing itself took.

Lucky for me, though, I took pretty good notes—right here, on this blog, lol. Like I've said, it's called The Offsec Journal for a reason. I told Claude explicitly to stick to only the source material I handed it, which consisted of the eight blog posts that made up the whole testing "saga" of this project, as well as HackTheBox's Pentest Report Guide. Claude and I went section by section, and as I transferred it to my Google Doc, I read every word—all 10.7 thousand of them—myself to make sure that the report was as accurate to my testing and experience as possible. Rest assured: what you'll see in that PDF isn't AI slop; it's the result of me using AI as a genuine tool, rather than as a replacement for me and my big beautiful brain.

Besides, what will AI take over in the near future, if not the busywork?

That said, here's that giant report for you guys! This was genuinely a lot of fun.

And here's that GitHub repo as well.

With that, my friends, the loop has officially been closed, and the promise of my very first post on here has (to the best of my ability) been fulfilled. That's a really cool feeling! At the bottom of that very first post, I said:

"If you want to follow along, bookmark this site...Let's see where I can get in 8 months."

I wrote that post on the 18th of December last year. Well, surprise surprise: today is the 19th of August—exactly 8 months and a day later. I swear that wasn't intentional, lol

I think it's safe to say I've gotten pretty far from where I started.

Glad you all got to see it happen, too.
